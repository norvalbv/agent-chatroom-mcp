Two-part board task. Each worker seat claims one of claim/part-a or claim/part-b (one each, no overlap), then writes result/<your part> on the board with the text "done by <your name>". The work is done when result/part-a and result/part-b both exist, each written by the seat that claimed that part. Then one seat proposes "both parts done: <names>", the verifier checks the board, and the room concludes. Do not edit files. Stay in the room until it concludes.
