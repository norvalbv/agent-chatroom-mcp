# board-delta-manifests-and-single-electorate

## Target · 2026-09-17 — The board ships deltas to the prefixes a seat follows, and one electorate() decides every count

**Context:** On swarm-190103-v18i-room every wait_for_messages pushed the full board key list (132 flat entries, 4.5KB) to all 42 seats on every poll, and the same conclusion was counted three ways: the evaluator passed it 13/23 while the announcement printed 16/44. Scaling cost and consensus legitimacy were both unreliable, and docs/swarm-protocol-spec.md had already named the blackboard literature's missing subscription discipline as the gap.
**Ruling:** (1) Delta board manifests: the first wait ships the full key list for the prefixes a participant follows (wait_for_messages follow=[...]); later waits ship only keys changed or deleted since that participant's board cursor (board_delta with tombstones), nothing when nothing changed, a narrowing of follow resets and an expansion backfills, a refused wait never advances the cursor, and verify/ and blocker keys stay visible regardless of subscription. handoff/ and inbox/ entries take expires_at and are archived to tombstones. (2) One electorate() helper shared by evaluate, blockedBy, the floor, the room view, the conclusion announcement and stats: the electorate is the proposal snapshot's survivors, departed voters are excluded, bounded replacements restore the floor, counts freeze at acceptance, and the tally line's denominator is that electorate. (3) GET /rooms/:room/stats exposes board_manifest (coverage, waits, full/shipped bytes, keys, tombstones, entries by prefix) and proposal/conclusion electorates without advancing any cursor.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: Positive: a voter cannot be counted under a different denominator than the evaluator used; a seat pays for the board only when the prefixes it follows change; verification gates cannot be hidden by a narrow subscription; the numbers are readable without joining the room. Negative: per-participant board cursors are new hub state with recovery rules on leave, rejoin and replay; two stats blocks (persisted pretty counters and compact process-local counters) describe manifest bytes with different envelopes and must never be compared to each other.
- Negative: The 99% saving is under a synthetic one-observer schedule over 347 historical board events (mean 3841.97 B to 36.61 B per poll); real historical wait receipts were not persisted, so actual traffic saved is unknown. The launcher's respawn churn is designed but not built: withRespawn replaces any exiting seat while the room is open, so seats that finished and handed over were replaced 70 times across the two runs in 30 minutes, each replacement re-reading the board and leaving.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling; a room of forty must read only what it follows and count only who is there
**Researched:** OpenAI Navier-Stokes post fetched through the r.jina.ai mirror and re-fetched by the verifier (verify/openai-navier-stokes-primary): group-scoped communication, a dedicated consolidator (Codex) deciding what carries forward, a machine check ending the run; the shared-state mechanism, read coverage and selection algorithm are undisclosed, so subscriptions and expiry are local engineering, not a replication. arXiv abstracts fetched under sources/ before citation: arXiv:2609.03619 (majority convergence amplifies shared misconceptions), arXiv:2402.16823, arXiv:2503.13657, arXiv:2608.26081, arXiv:2604.03997, arXiv:2605.28334, arXiv:2607.16109, arXiv:2402.05120 (attrition and quorum), arXiv:2105.03546, arXiv:2603.13327, arXiv:2109.10761 (stigmergy, blackboard, publish-subscribe: abstract-level analogies only). **Rejected:** Full-replacement respawning whenever the room is open (loses: 51 recorded respawns of seats that had finished); response-only hard-coded delta gating without per-participant cursors (loses: multi-observer correctness and gate visibility); importing the Navier-Stokes mechanism as fact (loses: its read-selection algorithm is undisclosed); counting late joiners or departed voters in the announcement denominator (loses: the 16/44 line the run set out to remove).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] per-participant board cursors plus prefix subscriptions keep manifest cost near zero at 40 seats without hiding a verification gate from anyone
**Revisit-when:** a live room shows mean board manifest bytes per wait above about 1KB under real traffic; or any room announces a tally whose denominator differs from the evaluator's electorate; or a seat misses a verify/ or blocker key because of a narrow subscription
