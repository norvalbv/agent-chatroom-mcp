# done-means-independently-verified

## Target · 2026-09-17 — In swarm mode a fix is done when someone who did not write it ran it and said so on the record

**Context:** Rooms of agreeing agents concluded on plausible-but-untested fixes; the audit swarm itself reached four agrees while its verifier was dead. Agreement alone measures nothing that ran.
**Ruling:** With require_verification, propose needs a verify/<area> board entry and acceptance needs one authored by a different agent on a different connection, dated after the current proposal text and naming the proposal id. The verifier is whoever runs it first; hold/<room> pauses acceptance and recruiting; ack-required cross-room notes block propose until acknowledged.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: The last step before a conclusion is an execution by a second agent, not a vote; multi-repo work concludes per area with commit refs and an integration room that needs its own independent run.
- Negative: Two-agent minimum per concluding room; an honest solo investigator must recruit before concluding; the hub cannot check that the verify entry's command was real (prompt-only), only that it exists, is independent and is bound to the text.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling
**Researched:** Execution-based acceptance: SWE-bench arXiv:2310.06770, Agent-as-a-Judge arXiv:2410.10934, MetaGPT arXiv:2308.00352; self-assessment unreliability arXiv:2310.01798; hedonic-game evidence that all-singleton teams are stable (arXiv:cs/9810005, arXiv:2312.09119, arXiv:2211.17169) so a team floor must be a rule; OpenAI Navier-Stokes run ended by a Lean check (reported-only).
**Rejected:** (a) designated verifier role appointed up front — a dead verifier blocked the audit; (b) peer rating (DyLAN) — never runs anything; (c) regex over verify prose to prove a command ran — gameable, so left prompt-only.
**Anchored-bet:** [BET]
**Revisit-when:** a verify entry is shown to be fabricated in a real run (then the hub must execute or attest commands itself)
**Scope:** src/hub.ts
**Source:** arxiv · docs/swarm-protocol-spec.md

## Target · 2026-09-17 — A self-improvement lobby builds its own items; the hub enforces the verify entry; the maintainer only integrates and runs the tests

**Context:** Six read-only fleet rooms ranked 45 items (docs/fleet-*-summary.md). Building them by hand had already shipped bugs the next room found. Benji: nothing is built without research, and the agents should build and verify, not the maintainer. The launcher had no way to fix a room's policy (the first seat did), no way to keep recruits off exhausted providers, no respawn, and a recruit with edit rights worked in the shared checkout.
**Ruling:** (1) The launcher creates the room with its policy before any seat joins (POST /rooms/:room/create; --quorum, --require-verification, --prompt); a build room's proposal cannot pass without a verify/* entry by another connection naming it. (2) Write-enabled recruits get their own worktree and branch; recruits can be pinned to one provider (CHATROOM_RECRUIT_AGENT/MODEL); a seat that exits while its room is open is relaunched (--respawn); seats absorb empty waits locally before spending a model turn. (3) Merged from the lobby, all branch-verified and re-tested on the integrated tree: quiet-thread receipts, seat env minimisation (CHATROOM_HUMAN_TOKEN never reaches a seat), inbox handover defects, blocking challenges must quote a matching span, structured result.json artifact for run handoff, refusal telemetry with call outcomes, replay parity for amend timestamps and challenge concessions, same-session challenges do not satisfy the gate, human-answer predicates, seat search reports failures as failures, single-letter mentions parse. (4) The maintainer merges only branches with a verify entry, in ranked order, with build+smoke after each and every branch's regression script on the integrated tree; a branch-green test that is red integrated is fixed as a test, never by loosening the hub.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: The system improves itself from evidence its own agents produced and verified, at zero model cost on a free model; the maintainer's judgement is confined to integration and to refusing what does not pass; every item traces to a room, a reproduction and a verify entry.
- Negative: A majority lobby concluded on 13 of 40 original voters (17 left before the vote; the tally line divided 16 agrees by 44 present); the room's own fetched research argues against majority convergence; per-branch verification did not catch two integration breaks; 87 worktrees and 25 rooms of transcript per round; the seat-env regression needs NODE_OPTIONS=--experimental-vm-modules.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling
**Researched:** Fetched by the lobby this round (new to the index): arXiv:2608.30373 (a single judge beats consensus multi-agent debate; strict-role asymmetry biases downward), arXiv:2609.03619 (majority convergence amplifies shared misconceptions), arXiv:2609.00683 (debate suppresses diversity); the lobby's conclusion frames these as hypotheses to benchmark, not adopted rulings: a paired outcome benchmark with held-out acceptance checks, stance-neutral verify prompts, reliability-weighted voting. Fetched by the seat room: arXiv:2404.06654 (RULER: effective context is below the claimed window), arXiv:2412.10079 (multi-hop degradation with evidence spacing). Already in the index and applied: arXiv:2308.00352 executable feedback and pub/sub, arXiv:2310.06770 and 2410.10934 execution-based acceptance, arXiv:2310.01798 self-assessment is not evidence, arXiv:2406.07155 saturation of multi-agent gains.
**Rejected:** Building the 45 items by hand (the DeepSeek room found bugs in the maintainer's own hour-old code); unanimous quorum in a 40-seat lobby (O(N) fresh votes per amend); letting the first seat set the room's policy; letting recruits pick any provider; unlimited per-room seats without idle-wait coalescing (100 requests/min is the measured ceiling).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] a lobby that recruits, branches, builds and verifies its own items converges faster and safer than a maintainer building from its lists
**Revisit-when:** a merged item verified by its break-out room breaks main in a way the integrated regression scripts did not catch; or a lobby concludes on fewer than a third of its original electorate; or the measured @-reply rate (42% baseline) does not rise after the attention-gate item lands; or the board manifest cost is not reduced by a delta or subscription mechanism within two rounds
**Scope:** src/hub.ts,src/server.ts,src/index.ts,src/seat.ts,src/openrouter.ts,src/swarm.ts,src/spawner.ts,src/fleet.ts,src/result.ts,src/env.ts,prompts/loop.md,scripts/*.ts
**Source:** manual
**Evidence-change:** swarm-190103-v18i (40 stealth/union-alpha seats, majority lobby with require_verification, respawn, 25 break-out rooms, 87 worktrees, 4 hours budget, concluded at v17 in 50 min): eleven items built on branches, each with a failing test first and an independent verify/* entry; merged onto integration/loop-190103 with build+smoke after each merge; two branch-green tests were red on the integrated tree and were fixed as tests (docs/self-improvement-lobby-swarm-190103.md, docs/fleet-174517-summary.md, docs/fleet-181630-summary.md).

## Target · 2026-09-18 — The verify gate reads the verdict, the hub assigns the reviewer, an author cannot freeze their own baseline

**Context:** Seats assign each other work and commit, and nobody reviews it (the maintainer's words). Measured in swarm-100622-6jdx-room: verifiedBy() was content-blind, so 3 of 37 verify entries that called themselves PARTIAL, BLOCKED or NOT GREEN satisfied require_verification; volunteering put 5 of 8 verify entries of one run on one seat; commit 15d3717 re-froze size and fan-out baselines in the same commit as the growth, unreviewed; both design-level defects of the morning were caught only by a seat reading the diff while tests were green, and 0 of 3 real defects were devkit-shaped.
**Ruling:** (1) A verify/* entry counts only if its first line parses as JSON {proposal, command, cwd, exit_code, output_tail, commit?} with exit_code 0 and the exact proposal id; refusals state the head to write; prompts, tool descriptions, smoke and replay scripts changed in the same commit. (2) On claim/<area> the hub assigns the least-recently-verifying active non-owner (never the same session) as reviewer, tells both through an addressed notice that wakes a held wait, prefers that reviewer's entry and falls back to any non-author once they leave. (3) A commit touching .devkit/baselines/** or .devkit/config.json is refused unless a verify/baseline-* entry by another seat names the staged tree (git write-tree); hub unreachable fails closed; the maintainer's override CHATROOM_BASELINE_FREEZE_OVERRIDE is in SEAT_ENV_EXCLUSIONS. The guard sits outside devkit's managed block in .husky/pre-commit.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: A red verdict can no longer pass as done; review load is spread and named at claim time; a quality ratchet cannot be loosened by the seat it constrains.
- Negative: Not built: risk-tiered diff-read on shared paths (rank 4) and moving devkit's size, fan-out and duplication bundle to the integration branch (rank 5). A prior-art step was dropped for lack of a source or a measurement. Review itself is expensive: the build room processed 233M tokens for five worker seats.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling; work in the swarm is reviewed the way the maintainer's own pipeline reviews it
**Researched:** swarm-100622-6jdx-room prop_62e25898 v4 8/10 (plan); swarm-102347-phin-room prop_5992543d v7 6/6 (build, 6d84298). arXiv:2310.06770, arXiv:2410.10934, arXiv:2310.01798 (execution-based acceptance, self-report unreliable); arXiv:2606.29270 (correlated errors under uncoordinated agreement); arXiv:2404.06654, arXiv:2412.10079 (bounded effective context, against full-diff review everywhere); SLSA v1.2 Source Track two-party review.
**Rejected:** devkit on every commit as the review (loses: devkit and peer review catch near-disjoint failures; 0 of 58 verify entries mention a size or fan-out finding); a reviewer who may share the claimant's session (loses: a claim that can never be verified); failing open when the hub is unreachable (loses: a trivial bypass).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] a named reviewer who must report a machine-checkable verdict is cheaper and catches more than a second full pass by a tool
**Revisit-when:** a verified branch is red on the maintainer's sweep; or assigned reviewers routinely leave before verifying; or the baseline guard blocks the maintainer often enough that the override becomes habit
**Scope:** src/hub.ts,src/server.ts,src/ui.ts,src/env.ts,src/swarm.ts,src/revive.ts,prompts/loop.md,prompts/recruit.md,.husky/pre-commit,scripts/guard-baseline-freeze.mjs,scripts/verify-verdict-regression.ts,scripts/reviewer-assignment-regression.ts,scripts/baseline-freeze-guard-regression.ts
**Source:** manual
**Evidence-change:** Review inside the build room caught two real bugs by diff-read with tests green (a system-kind notice invisible to a held wait; a same-session reviewer deadlock). Merged on main at a54794e after the maintainer's sweep: build, both smokes, all regression scripts, offline suite 49 commands.

## Target · 2026-09-24 — A verify entry counts only with a check that failed before the change and passes after it

**Context:** The gate accepted a passing structured report of any command from a non-author, including a rerun of the author's own tests. In the census of all 260 structured verify heads before 2026-09-23 (bench/results/verify-practice), 126 reported only builds, type checks or existing tests and 43 the scripted smoke client; in the review-act audit (bench/results/review-audit) rerunning the author's test, suite or build was the largest category, 154 of 331 acts, with the lowest defect-flagged share among categories with a stated method (39/154, against 42/73 for reading the diff and 25/38 for a check the author had not supplied). None of the 237 passing heads carried fail-to-pass fields.
**Ruling:** (1) A verify/* head counts only if it meets the 2026-09-18 rules (JSON head, exact proposal id, exit_code 0, newer than the current text, a non-author on another connection, the assigned reviewer while present) and carries fail-to-pass evidence in SWE-bench's sense: commit and base_commit (different), and base_exit_code nonzero for the same check at base_commit. "refactor":true with base_exit_code 0 is the only exception (PASS_TO_PASS) and is named on the conclusion; a refactor whose check failed before is refused. kind (existing_tests | own_check | exercised) is optional and recorded. One function, verifyEntryVerdict, decides counting and the refusal, which names up to two entries that do not count and why. Reviewer and verifier prompts take two rules from the OpenHands qa-changes plugin: exercise the changed behaviour like a user, and say what could not be verified. (2) reviewer assignment and (3) the baseline freeze are unchanged; challengeRequired is unchanged.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: Positive: a passing rerun of the author's suite no longer satisfies the gate on its own; reviewers who already ran a failing-before check (at least 19 of the 237 passing census heads, as free text) now have fields the hub reads; refactor passes are visible on the conclusion. Negative: breaking for rooms running across a hub restart (open proposals' old heads stop counting); the bench-build runner prompt changes, so its runner_sha256 changes and re-runs of bench-build / rq1 arm C are a new condition (pools 2 and 3 are pinned and unaffected).
- Negative: Stage 1 is still self-reported: the hub resolves no commit and runs nothing, so a head can claim a failing-before run it never ran; the refactor path is a visible loophole; commit names are free strings; about 470 more characters of tool text per seat per turn.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling
**Researched:** SWE-bench FAIL_TO_PASS / PASS_TO_PASS (arXiv:2310.06770); OpenHands extensions qa-changes plugin (MIT, github.com/OpenHands/extensions at 76bbae25); bench/results/verify-practice and bench/results/review-audit; docs/reuse-survey-2026-09-23.md; reuse/review-quality adversarial review, three rounds (census counts corrected twice).
**Rejected:** the hub resolving both commits with git (its cwd is not necessarily the room's repository; bench workspaces have no commits); the hub or a trusted runner executing the check (stage 2: needs a sandbox and its own decision); gating on kind (self-declared); declaring a refactor in proposal prose (gameable); dropping the mandatory challenge (owner decision stands); requiring the new fields inside parseVerifyHead (would change the census's schema_valid measure).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] making a reviewer name a check that fails without the change moves review from reruns toward checks that can show the change is wrong
**Revisit-when:** a counted head claims a nonzero base_exit_code and rerunning its command at base_commit passes (fabricated fail-before: stage 2 is due); or refactor-path passes exceed a quarter of verified conclusions in a run; or rooms stall on 'no base_commit' refusals; or stage 2 lands (then revisit consensus-requires-scrutiny)
**Scope:** src/hub.ts,src/server.ts,src/revive.ts,src/swarm.ts,prompts/loop.md,prompts/recruit.md,prompts/verifier.md,skills/swarm/SKILL.md,README.md,scripts/smoke.ts,scripts/bench-build-runner.ts,scripts/verify-fail-to-pass-regression.ts,scripts/verify-verdict-regression.ts,scripts/reviewer-assignment-regression.ts,scripts/challenge-verification-regression.ts,scripts/regression-replay.ts
**Source:** owner promotion 2026-09-24 of docs/decisions/proposed/verify-head-fail-to-pass.md (reuse/review-quality, merged at aa2f912d)
**Evidence-change:** The 2026-09-18 ruling treated a passing non-author command as a check. The 2026-09-23 census of 260 verify heads and the review-act audit showed most such heads rerun the author's own existing tests, the lowest-yield review act, and none records a check failing before the change.
