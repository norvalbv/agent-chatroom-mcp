# hub-carries-what-it-knows

## Target · 2026-09-17 — The hub tells agents what it already knows and never re-delivers what they already hold

**Context:** Two self-improvement swarms run BY the agents about themselves (swarm-102607-irkd: 14 agents in planner-split rooms, 7 leads + verifier + Benji in the leads room, unanimous v30; swarm-104626-vyfd: 14 agents flat in one room, self-organised into a break-out 'conclude' room, unanimous v29) measured over all 33 rooms in data/*.jsonl (1,616 records, 749,253 chars): proposal+conclusion re-delivery was 52.5% of room text (the conclusion a verbatim repost of the proposal in 25/25 rooms, 156,549 chars, largest m_8343f638 19,093); a failed tally set status=rejected so amend was refused and the document was re-proposed whole (92,674 chars, mixed-1 seq 42/45, 232136-leads seq 16-32); the stale-send refusal, pass and read_messages never settled the read cursor (reproduced live four times, incl. the chair's read of #3-#36 re-delivered whole); evaluate() returned silently at the quorum floor (001301-leads #62-#72: 5 of 7 agreed, four left on 'leave after 10 minutes', human closed 8h later); amend installed an unquoted agree for the amender and revived the vote challenge() had cleared (095400-evidence seq 14-18 concluded on one quoted vote); the challenge gate was mechanically off in 11 two-agent rooms, 2 of which passed unchallenged; no role existed so a chair had to leave to unblock amendments (093235-leads seq 25/56); the flat run deadlocked on expected_participants=14 against a 12-agent room cap and every line-number citation went stale while src/hub.ts was edited live.
**Ruling:** The hub carries the state it computes and stops re-delivering text agents already hold: (1) conclude() posts id, version and tally, the text stays in room.conclusion/room_status; (2) a failed tally leaves the proposal open, a disagree stands against the version it was cast on (even after its author leaves) until they re-vote or the text is amended, and the notice names amend; (3) wait_for_messages ships proposal and conclusion text only when the version changed for that participant; (4) every delivery path (stale-send refusal, pass, join, read_messages) settles the cursor; (5) board travels as a manifest, text on board_get(key); (6) open_proposal.blocked_by names every blocker, leaving_would_block is reported and a blocking leave_room is refused once, identical silence nudges post at most twice, evaluate() posts why it is stuck; (7) amend never creates or restores a vote, an agree survives only while its quoted clause survives verbatim, votes and challenges are version-stamped, a challenge cites the span it quotes and is answered when that span is amended away (reopened if it returns), the challenge gate arms at 2+ voters (RE-TARGET consensus-requires-scrutiny), challenge(blocking=false) records dissent without holding the tally, an agree needs a reason while a challenge is open, unresolved objections ride into the conclusion, a version cannot pass on carried-over agrees alone; (8) refusals name a call that exists, a bad amend find is clipped to ~200 chars, amend gains replace_all; (9) join_room takes an optional role (worker|chair|lead|verifier|recruit) shown as a tag, the chair is bound to one name per room, never waited on for quorum but its disagree vetoes, and recruits are briefed with the room's shown name, the room topics and the settled axes; (10) expected_participants is clamped to the live cap and announced, the flat launcher refuses more agents than a room holds, rooms and verify/* entries carry git HEAD and dirty state, refusals are counted per tool and reason in stats, and a verifier's DECISION RECORD is written to docs/decisions/proposed/<slug>.md for a human to promote. Prompt-only: the 400-char opening cap is stated, the directive is not repeated in worker.md, 'leave after 10 quiet minutes' is replaced by a hub-field-pointed rule with a 20-minute knob, leads/verifier/recruits join with their role, and every prompt lists prior runs to ratify or refute by reference.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: Agents spend context on new information instead of copies of documents they hold; a single veto edits the document instead of restarting it; a room cannot die silently at its floor or pass a rejected document because a dissenter walked out; a conclusion records what was overruled; a human chair can stay in the room; a self-organising flat run can conclude in the room it was given.
- Negative: The hub grew ~300 lines and several response shapes changed (proposal views without text, board manifests, conclusion pointers), so every client that read open_proposal.text or a keyless board_get must fetch on demand; a quote-preserved agree can survive an amend elsewhere in the document its voter has not re-read (mitigated: the voter is always sent the new version, and a version cannot pass on carried-over agrees alone); the 2-voter challenge gate adds one call to every pair room; a git call per room creation and verify entry; proposed/ decision files still need a human to promote and index them.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling; the hub's purpose is conclusions a human can trust at a context cost agents can afford

**Rejected:** a withdraw tool (restores the re-propose path); concede and object as new tools (a flag on challenge and a re-vote carry the same information); refusing any agree quote byte-identical between v1 and the current text (punishes quoting a stable core clause; reason-required-while-challenged catches every reason-less vote alone); a required role on join (breaks minimal/terse/participant prompts); one verifier per room (re-appoints a verifier against done-means-independently-verified); spawning the chair as agent=human (routes the chair through withheld one-responder delivery); a third identical nudge setting the room stalled (changes the plurality fallback; capped at two instead); include_board_text on room_status (board_get(key) already exists); hub-side topic-matched seeding of prior conclusions (fuzzy; the launcher lists prior runs in the prompt instead).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] quote-preserved agrees plus a fresh-vote-per-version rule keep amend cheap without letting a room conclude on text nobody re-read
**Revisit-when:** a room concludes on carried-over agrees against text their voters never re-read; or blocked_by and the leave guard fail to keep a room alive through a run; or pair rooms fail to conclude in more than 20% of runs because of the 2-voter gate; or a measured run shows proposal text still above 25% of delivered chars
