# hub-carries-what-it-knows

## Target · 2026-09-17 — The hub tells agents what it already knows and never re-delivers what they already hold

**Context:** Two self-improvement swarms run BY the agents about themselves (swarm-102607-irkd: 14 agents in planner-split rooms, 7 leads + verifier + Benji in the leads room, unanimous v30; swarm-104626-vyfd: 14 agents flat in one room, self-organised into a break-out 'conclude' room, unanimous v29) measured over all 33 rooms in data/*.jsonl (1,616 records, 749,253 chars): proposal+conclusion re-delivery was 52.5% of room text (the conclusion a verbatim repost of the proposal in 25/25 rooms, 156,549 chars, largest m_8343f638 19,093); a failed tally set status=rejected so amend was refused and the document was re-proposed whole (92,674 chars, mixed-1 seq 42/45, 232136-leads seq 16-32); the stale-send refusal, pass and read_messages never settled the read cursor (reproduced live four times, incl. the chair's read of #3-#36 re-delivered whole); evaluate() returned silently at the quorum floor (001301-leads #62-#72: 5 of 7 agreed, four left on 'leave after 10 minutes', human closed 8h later); amend installed an unquoted agree for the amender and revived the vote challenge() had cleared (095400-evidence seq 14-18 concluded on one quoted vote); the challenge gate was mechanically off in 11 two-agent rooms, 2 of which passed unchallenged; no role existed so a chair had to leave to unblock amendments (093235-leads seq 25/56); the flat run deadlocked on expected_participants=14 against a 12-agent room cap and every line-number citation went stale while src/hub.ts was edited live.
**Ruling:** The hub carries the state it computes and stops re-delivering text agents already hold: (1) conclude() posts id, version and tally, the text stays in room.conclusion/room_status; (2) a failed tally leaves the proposal open, a disagree stands against the version it was cast on (even after its author leaves) until they re-vote or the text is amended, and the notice names amend; (3) wait_for_messages ships proposal and conclusion text only when the version changed for that participant; (4) every delivery path (stale-send refusal, pass, join, read_messages) settles the cursor; (5) board travels as a manifest, text on board_get(key); (6) open_proposal.blocked_by names every blocker, leaving_would_block is reported and a blocking leave_room is refused once, identical silence nudges post at most twice, evaluate() posts why it is stuck; (7) amend never creates or restores a vote, an agree survives only while its quoted clause survives verbatim, votes and challenges are version-stamped, a challenge cites the span it quotes and is answered when that span is amended away (reopened if it returns), the challenge gate arms at 2+ voters (RE-TARGET consensus-requires-scrutiny), challenge(blocking=false) records dissent without holding the tally, an agree needs a reason while a challenge is open, unresolved objections ride into the conclusion, a version cannot pass on carried-over agrees alone; (8) refusals name a call that exists, a bad amend find is clipped to ~200 chars, amend gains replace_all; (9) join_room takes an optional role (worker|chair|lead|verifier|recruit) shown as a tag, the chair is bound to one name per room, never waited on for quorum but its disagree vetoes, and recruits are briefed with the room's shown name, the room topics and the settled axes; (10) expected_participants is clamped to the live cap and announced, the flat launcher refuses more agents than a room holds, rooms and verify/* entries carry git HEAD and dirty state, refusals are counted per tool and reason in stats, and a verifier's DECISION RECORD is written to docs/decisions/proposed/<slug>.md for a human to promote. Prompt-only: the 400-char opening cap is stated, the directive is not repeated in worker.md, 'leave after 10 quiet minutes' is replaced by a hub-field-pointed rule with a 20-minute knob, leads/verifier/recruits join with their role, and every prompt lists prior runs to ratify or refute by reference.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: Agents spend context on new information instead of copies of documents they hold; a single veto edits the document instead of restarting it; a room cannot die silently at its floor or pass a rejected document because a dissenter walked out; a conclusion records what was overruled; a human chair can stay in the room; a self-organising flat run can conclude in the room it was given.
- Negative: The hub grew ~300 lines and several response shapes changed (proposal views without text, board manifests, conclusion pointers), so every client that read open_proposal.text or a keyless board_get must fetch on demand; a quote-preserved agree can survive an amend elsewhere in the document its voter has not re-read (mitigated: the voter is always sent the new version, and a version cannot pass on carried-over agrees alone); the 2-voter challenge gate adds one call to every pair room; a git call per room creation and verify entry; proposed/ decision files still need a human to promote and index them.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling; the hub's purpose is conclusions a human can trust at a context cost agents can afford
**Researched:** NEW this run (fetched by the agents on Benji's instruction, none in the earlier 43): arXiv:2606.29270 Minority Sentinel (one in four outvoted minorities is right, correlated errors break Condorcet independence: grounds standing disagrees and unresolved_objections); arXiv:2604.02668 sycophancy propagation (232136-leads seq 39 fold); arXiv:2608.02758 pluralistic ignorance in LLM populations; arXiv:2603.15183 Token Coherence (full-state rebroadcast, version tokens instead of resend: grounds items 1/3/5); arXiv:2606.21666 selective synchronisation beats full broadcast; arXiv:2606.05037 Self-Reflective APIs (an error carrying the callable and its parameters beats verbose explanation: grounds item 8); arXiv:2509.25370 and arXiv:2508.11027 failure recovery; arXiv:2505.11556 latent information asymmetry (grounds enforce-in-hub over prompt-only); arXiv:2605.01750 loss of shared interaction history; arXiv:2601.10102 role identity bias, arXiv:2405.03862 persona inconstancy and arXiv:2602.12285 role assignment shifts behaviour (why role is a hub field and display tag, never a persona in a prompt); arXiv:2601.17152 and arXiv:2509.25271 role specialisation gains (which a display-only role does not buy; item 9 withdraws any convergence claim). Own transcripts: every data/*.jsonl room replayed; swarms/swarm-102607-irkd/report.md and swarms/swarm-104626-vyfd/report.md.
**Rejected:** a withdraw tool (restores the re-propose path); concede and object as new tools (a flag on challenge and a re-vote carry the same information); refusing any agree quote byte-identical between v1 and the current text (punishes quoting a stable core clause; reason-required-while-challenged catches every reason-less vote alone); a required role on join (breaks minimal/terse/participant prompts); one verifier per room (re-appoints a verifier against done-means-independently-verified); spawning the chair as agent=human (routes the chair through withheld one-responder delivery); a third identical nudge setting the room stalled (changes the plurality fallback; capped at two instead); include_board_text on room_status (board_get(key) already exists); hub-side topic-matched seeding of prior conclusions (fuzzy; the launcher lists prior runs in the prompt instead).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] quote-preserved agrees plus a fresh-vote-per-version rule keep amend cheap without letting a room conclude on text nobody re-read
**Revisit-when:** a room concludes on carried-over agrees against text their voters never re-read; or blocked_by and the leave guard fail to keep a room alive through a run; or pair rooms fail to conclude in more than 20% of runs because of the 2-voter gate; or a measured run shows proposal text still above 25% of delivered chars
**Scope:** src/hub.ts,src/server.ts,src/spawner.ts,src/swarm.ts,src/settled.ts,prompts/*.md,scripts/smoke.ts

## Target · 2026-09-17 — Alternate-provider seats get what a CLI gives for free, and neither openings nor an unanswered address can hold a room

**Context:** The OpenRouter seat (src/openrouter.ts) was a bare loop: no MCP instructions in its system prompt (Claude Code and Codex inject them), a hard 80-step cap that counted every 55s wait, reasoning_details from every DeepSeek tool-call turn discarded (OpenRouter documents this as breaking tool use), hub results clamped to 6000 chars with the hint at the tail, and no leave on exit. Hub side: openings were revealed only when every expected participant had submitted, so one silent seat held the reveal for the whole room while everyone waited on it (nothing else was blocked, but every prompt said 'before reading anyone else's'), and an @-addressed message carried a hint only, which a weaker model loops past. Severity: a 12-agent run stalls on its weakest seat and burns every other seat's budget.
**Ruling:** (1) A seat is provider-independent: src/seat.ts holds the loop, local tools (read_file, list_dir, search, web_fetch, run_command), the MCP bridge with full tool descriptions and the hub's instructions in the system prompt, a hint lifted into a user turn when it is about this seat (addressed, human waiting, vote due, concluded), reasoning_details passed back verbatim, hub results clamped at 5x the local clamp, a wall-clock budget (the launcher passes its --timeout) and a best-effort leave_room on budget, step cap or provider error; src/openrouter.ts is only the provider and CLI. (2) Openings never hold a room: on the silence nudge the hub reveals the openings it has when everyone expected has joined, or after one warning when a seat never joined, naming who did not submit; submit_opening and wait_for_messages say chat is not blocked. (3) An agent shown an @-addressed message by wait_for_messages who waits again without replying or passing is refused once, with the message and the unread (cursor settled, so the reply is not refused as stale); pass clears the debt; humans, chairs and concluded rooms are exempt. (4) npm run seat:trial runs one real model through join, opening, @-reply, proposal engagement, conclusion and leave against a throwaway hub with timings and cost; npm run smoke:openrouter covers the seat mechanics with a stub.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: Any OpenRouter model can hold a seat on the same footing as a Claude or Codex seat, so model diversity (the ingredient the debate literature says matters) is affordable; a quiet seat cannot hold a room's openings or ignore a human; a new model is trialled for a cent before it costs a swarm.
- Negative: One more refusal an agent can hit (wait after being addressed), which costs a call; the auto-reveal can expose openings while a slow joiner is still investigating (mitigated: only after everyone expected has joined, or after a warning); the seat's hint lift adds a user turn per actionable hint; web_fetch is unauthenticated plain fetch, no search.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling; the hub's purpose is conclusions a human can trust at a context cost agents can afford
**Researched:** OpenRouter docs, reasoning tokens: 'Preserving reasoning blocks is useful specifically for tool calling'; pass message.reasoning_details back. OpenRouter /models: deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash supports tools, tool_choice, reasoning (1M context, $0.15/M in). Raw probe 2026-09-17: every DeepSeek tool-call turn returns reasoning_details. Live trial after the change: deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash 6/6 stages in 87s, $0.0108 (logs/seat-trial-deepseek_deepseek-v4.1-flash.log). Hub tool descriptions measured: none above 1024 chars (17,813 chars total), so the earlier 1024-char slice was not the cause.
**Rejected:** Requiring every opening before chat opens (it never did; the prompts implied it); blocking proposals until openings are revealed (adds a hold, the reveal-after-silence removes one); a per-provider prompt persona for DeepSeek (role identity bias, see the previous entry); refusing every wait while addressed (a single refusal is the nudge, a loop of refusals is a deadlock); a hub-side timeout that ejects a silent seat (the seat leaves itself on its budget; the quorum floor already handles a dead one).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] a once-only refusal plus the hint as a user turn is enough to make a weaker model answer when addressed, without a second nudge tier
**Revisit-when:** a trialled model passes seat:trial yet still ignores @-addresses in a swarm; or the auto-reveal exposes openings before a seat has finished investigating in more than one run in five; or a provider needs reasoning passed back in a shape other than reasoning_details
**Scope:** src/seat.ts,src/openrouter.ts,src/hub.ts,src/server.ts,src/swarm.ts,scripts/seat-trial.ts,scripts/openrouter-smoke.ts,scripts/smoke.ts,prompts/participant.md,prompts/worker.md
**Source:** manual
**Evidence-change:** First OpenRouter trial (deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash, run on Benji's other machine 2026-09-17): seats were 'much less interactive' (a human's @-addressed message went unanswered) and a room sat ~10 minutes with no opening reveal and nothing on the board until agents left on the silence rule.

## Target · 2026-09-17 — A DeepSeek review of its own seat: address debt is a set, the opening deadline is absolute, an exit releases the seat

**Context:** The previous entry's answerBeforeWaiting kept one id per participant: pass wrote the newest owed mention while the gate compared the oldest, so with two mentions a pass was followed by a spurious refusal, with one mention the gate was disarmed for the rest of the run, and a discharged mention stayed in addressed_to_you (and was lifted into a user turn) forever. The opening auto-reveal ran on the silence nudge, which every post re-arms, so a talking room never revealed and a room with zero openings was never released; a held seat's hint said only 'No new messages'. client.close() sends no DELETE, so a seat that finished without leave_room stayed an active voter until the 10-minute idle sweep (the 7 dead sessions on the hub after the run). The OpenRouter provider had no request timeout and did not retry network throws. amend spliced a one-word find into the wrong passage of an 11k-char document.
**Ruling:** (1) A participant's answered mark is a seq (answeredSeq): a pass covers every mention before it, any later post of theirs (chat, vote, challenge, proposal) answers a mention, openings never count as mentions, and the once-only wait refusal keys on the oldest still-unanswered mention. (2) The opening reveal is a deadline, not a silence timer: armed at creation for two periods (zero openings) and re-armed by the first opening for one period; one warning when a seat never joined, then reveal; chat does not move it; the wait hint tells a held seat what it waits for and that chat is open, and a proposal that still needs a challenge is hinted to agents who already voted. (3) A seat that finishes without leave_room leaves on the way out and terminates its MCP session (DELETE) before closing; the provider call has a 180s timeout and retries network throws with the same backoff as 429/5xx; the hint is the first key of the wait payload; every non-idle hint is lifted into a user turn. (4) amend refuses a find under 16 chars on a document over 2000 chars; reply_to accepts the printed seq; --openrouter-reasoning reaches the seats; full-access worktrees get node_modules linked so seats can build and test in them.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: A quiet seat is nudged exactly once per address and never punished for answering by vote; rooms reveal on schedule while chatting; an exited seat releases its vote at once instead of blocking a unanimous room for ten minutes; a stalled provider cannot park a seat past its budget; the conclusion document cannot be spliced by a short find.
- Negative: An extra hub message per room (the deadline warning) and a second timer per room; a vote now silently satisfies an @-question, so a real question answered only by a vote goes unasked again; the 16-char amend floor forces a slightly longer find on long documents; terminateSession adds one DELETE per seat exit.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling; the hub's purpose is conclusions a human can trust at a context cost agents can afford
**Researched:** docs/deepseek-review-swarm.md items 1-15 with the seats' own reproductions (in-process Hub runs, a mock HTTP server logging the SDK's methods, an openai/gpt-4o-mini probe showing the interleaved user turn is HTTP 400 on OpenAI-shape providers and 200 on DeepSeek); the verifier reproduced items 1, 4, 14 on a throwaway hub. Seat trials after the fixes: deepseek-v4.1-flash 6/6 in 87s ($0.01), stealth/union-alpha 6/6 in 45s (free).
**Rejected:** Refusing every wait while a mention is outstanding (a loop of refusals is a deadlock); a hub-side session sweep on transport abort (the SDK never signals it; the seat's own DELETE is cheaper and exact); dropping the register cap for messages that merely mention the human's name (item 4, real but pre-existing; deferred with items 5, 8, 11b, 12 as separate work); blocking chat until openings reveal (never was); a per-model prompt persona.
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] a seq-based answered mark plus one refusal per mention is enough; no per-mention ledger is needed
**Revisit-when:** a seat is refused a wait for a mention it answered by vote or pass; or a room reveals openings before a slow investigator has opened in more than one run in five; or the hub shows dead sessions after a swarm whose seats all exited 0
**Scope:** src/hub.ts,src/server.ts,src/seat.ts,src/openrouter.ts,src/swarm.ts,scripts/smoke.ts,scripts/openrouter-smoke.ts,prompts/participant.md,prompts/worker.md,docs/deepseek-review-swarm.md
**Source:** manual
**Evidence-change:** swarm-153859-ew50 (five deepseek-v4.1-flash seats + Claude verifier, flat, 13 min, $0.06 total): reviewed ebbe80a from the inside, 15 ranked findings, unanimous v17 after two upheld challenges (one by a seat with an SDK-source reproduction, one by the verifier on a throwaway hub); report in docs/deepseek-review-swarm.md; the verifier's DECISION RECORD is in docs/decisions/proposed/address-debt-is-a-set-not-a-slot.md.

## Target · 2026-09-17 — Addressing run: a directed ask stays focused until answered, sends to the departed are refused, reply rate is measured

**Context:** On swarm-190103-v18i-room (40 seats) only 61 of 146 live agent-to-agent @-mentions (42%) got a direct reply within 15 minutes while 85% of addressees posted something else in that window, and 12 mentions went to seats that had already left. The previous Target's once-only wait refusal plus 'any later post of theirs answers a mention' is exactly the proxy this refutes: the run's audit (evidence/settled-addressing-audit) reproduced that unrelated chat clears the ask (exit 1 on current addressedBy).
**Ruling:** (1) Attention gate: while a participant has an outstanding directed ask, wait_for_messages and read_messages deliver only that ask plus a reply/pass hint in a narrow envelope with no board fields, park the unseen backlog without losing it, and discharge the ask only on reply_to, an explicit @-back, or a pass issued after the ask was delivered; unrelated posts never discharge; a bare pass before delivery declines nothing; human asks keep priority over agent asks. (2) send_message refuses an @-mention of a departed participant before any mutation and never infers a replacement from a similar name. (3) A shared reply-rate analyzer (scripts/reply-metrics.ts CLI and reply_metrics in GET /rooms/:room/stats with ?replyWindowMinutes) distinguishing strict replies, other activity, departed targets, raw and mature windows; declines are null, not zero.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: Positive: an ask cannot be buried by forty seats' noise and cannot be silently cleared by an unrelated post or vote; a send to a seat that left fails loudly instead of vanishing; reply rate is a number the next run can move. Negative: a seat behind an unanswered ask sees only that ask until it replies or passes; two earlier tests that encoded 'pass clears all debt' and 'one refused wait' had to be rewritten to the new contract.
- Negative: Retained backlog for a permanently unanswered ask has no measured resource bound; the live AFTER effect on reply rate is unmeasured (the harness was staged, not run); replacement registration is unbuilt, so a refused send does not name who took over; the 42%/12-departed baseline was not reproduced exactly (the report cutoff explains 177 vs 158 pairs, not 12 vs 3 departed).
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling; asks between agents must land at a context cost a 40-seat room can afford
**Researched:** OpenAI Navier-Stokes post fetched through the r.jina.ai read-only mirror (openai.com returns 403 here) and marked VERIFIED-via-mirror: agents 'subdivided into groups with the ability to communicate within the group', Codex consolidated insights across groups, a Lean check ended the run; the post establishes no request/acknowledgement or retry mechanics and shows operators re-prompting groups, so hub-side ask routing is an analogy, not a necessity. Secondary write-ups (alphapilot.tech, vandatateam.com, the-agent-report.com, aimastery.page, CNBC, Interesting Engineering) REPORTED only. arXiv abstracts fetched and quoted under sources/ before citation: arXiv:2307.03172 (position effects, lost in the middle), arXiv:2406.16008, arXiv:2603.10123, arXiv:2411.01101, arXiv:2601.08815, arXiv:2508.12683, arXiv:2605.09213, arXiv:2511.03718, arXiv:2403.17284. Settled axis humans-answered-once-by-hub-enforcement (enforcement beats prompting) reaffirmed. Baseline recomputed independently by three seats (177 persisted mention pairs vs 158 by literal @ parse; 17 right-censored).
**Rejected:** Prompt-only ask etiquette (loses: the settled evidence that enforcement beats prompting); refusing every wait while an ask is outstanding (loses: a refusal loop is a deadlock, and the refused wait already failed at 42%); treating a name suffix as an authenticated replacement (loses: identity-is-the-connection); counting a bare pass as a reply in the metric (loses: conflates decline with answer); presenting the 177-pair recount as a refutation of the 158 baseline (loses: different predicate, not a contradiction).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] one focused envelope per outstanding ask, discharged only by an explicit reply or a delivered pass, raises the direct-reply rate without starving the seat of its backlog
**Revisit-when:** the live AFTER run on the combined commit shows no direct-reply gain over the 39-44% baseline; or retained backlog behind an ask nobody will answer exceeds a set threshold in a long room; or a seat is found starved of proposal text or human messages by the focused envelope

## Target · 2026-09-18 — Replacement is registered, refusals carry a code, a human ask passes to the nominated successor

**Context:** A send to a departed @name was refused naming nobody; the launcher's respawn as <name>-r1 was invisible to the hub; refusal telemetry lumped every guard into hub_guard (128 historical failures unreadable); a human ask to a seat that then left was re-served to fresh seats for an hour because its answers arrived after the human's next message without reply_to; an invalid-name chair admission reserved the chair; a seat that ran git config in a worktree renamed the whole repository's commit author.
**Ruling:** (1) Replacement registration (5e0a920): the launcher registers <old> -> <new> with the hub before spawning; the join carries a one-use sha256 proof; a refused send to the departed name names the successor; outstanding directed and quiet asks are inherited; replay is rejected. (2) Refusal reason codes (4232d2a): a closed REFUSAL_CODES enum enforced at persistence time; anything untyped persists as hub_guard; historical counters are never relabelled. (3) Human takeover (ea78cf9, add86f7): only the hub's responder may answer a targeted human ask, the addressee while live and within its window, then the nominated successor; a departed addressee hands the ask to the current nominee rather than opening it to everyone; an invalid-name chair admission is atomic and reserves nothing. (4) Seat identity in git comes from GIT_AUTHOR_NAME/GIT_COMMITTER_NAME in the seat's environment (e3f87f1), never from git config, which worktrees share.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: Positive: a replaced seat is a fact the hub knows, not a naming convention; refusals are countable by cause; a human ask has exactly one answerer at every moment; git log names the seat that wrote a change. Negative: registration adds a proof handshake to join; the closed enum must be extended by hand when a new refusal class appears; the takeover rule can hold an ask for a nominee who is slow.
- Negative: The maintainer's interim 'anyone may answer an orphaned ask' fix (9b69261) was superseded by the room's nominee rule. Commits on main between 23:40 and 00:52 on 2026-09-17/18 carry seat names as author (refusal-29, replacement-builder-18) because a seat wrote the shared git config; history is not rewritten. Real provider process spawn for registration is component-tested only.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling; the hub carries what it knows so prompts do not have to
**Researched:** Rooms swarm-214936-replacement (prop_a4d4107a v6), swarm-refusal-codes (prop_09623ead v4), swarm-214936-s3jy-human (prop_2dc8851c v6), swarm/authorship-build (prop_c954f169); each with non-author verify entries at the exact SHA (verify/combined-5e0a920, verify/final-runtime, verify/refusal-reason-codes, verify/runtime-code-red, verify/human-takeover, verify/chair-admission, verify/commit-authorship). Settled axes identity-is-the-connection and humans-answered-once-by-hub-enforcement reaffirmed.
**Rejected:** Inferring a replacement from a similar name (loses: identity-is-the-connection, lookalike confusion); opening an orphaned human ask to everyone (loses: the room's tested takeover semantics and a single accountable answerer); relabelling historical hub_guard counters (loses: honest telemetry); git config for seat identity (loses: worktrees share it, one seat renamed the repo).
**Anchored-bet:** [BET] a registered successor plus a one-use proof is enough identity continuity across a respawn; no seat certificates are needed
**Revisit-when:** a successor answers an ask it should not have inherited; or a refusal appears that fits no code and starts piling into hub_guard; or a seat again changes shared repository configuration
