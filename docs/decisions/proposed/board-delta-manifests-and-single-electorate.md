DECISION RECORD for the decision log (guard-decisions add):
- slug: board-delta-manifests-and-single-electorate
- context (forcing failure and cost): On swarm-190103-v18i-room every wait_for_messages pushed the full 4.5KB board key list (132 flat entries) to all 42 seats, and the electorate was computed three different ways — evaluator 13/23 vs announced 16/44 for the same conclusion — making both scaling cost and consensus legitimacy unreliable.
- ruling (the mechanism): Delta board manifests with per-participant delivery cursors and prefix subscriptions (first wait full, changes/deletions only after, no-change zero bytes, tombstones, narrowing resets with expansion backfill, refusal-safe cursors, persisted/archived handoff and inbox expiry), plus one electorate() helper shared by evaluate, blockedBy, the floor, view, announcement and stats, with accepted-count freezing at proposal acceptance, departed-voter exclusion, bounded replacements, participant-weighted tally, and a stats board_manifest block exposing coverage/waits/bytes/keys/tombstones/prefix counts without advancing cursors.
- consequences (value protected): A voter cannot be silently counted under a different denominator than the one the evaluator used; peers stop paying full-manifest cost per poll; verification and scrutiny gates stay visible regardless of subscription narrowing.
- tradeoff (cost knowingly paid): Per-participant cursors add hub state and recovery rules (leave/rejoin, replay resets delivery cursors); delta savings are schedule-dependent — 99.047% under the measured synthetic schedule, actual historical traffic still unknown; the respawn-churn fix (completed/error/orphaned-work states, bounded retries, verifier/floor preservation) is designed but not built.
- researched (this run): verify/openai-navier-stokes-primary (re-fetched primary via proxy, confirming shape only) — already read; arXiv 2609.03619, 2402.16823, 2503.13657, 2608.26081, 2604.03997, 2605.28334, 2607.16109, 2402.05120, 2105.03546, 2603.13327, 2109.10761 — all NEW relative to the settled-axes source list, recorded under sources/<id> before citation.
- rejected: (a) full-replacement respawning on room=open — loses completed-slice seat stability (51 recorded respawns); (b) response-only hard-coded delta gating — loses gate visibility and multi-observer correctness vs the implemented cursor/delta contract; (c) importing Navier-Stokes mechanisms as fact — loses on grounding, its read-selection algorithm is undisclosed, so subscriptions/expiry stay local engineering; (d) keeping the stale draft/ranked-list as incorporated content — loses on retracted-figure exclusion.
- revisit-when: a live-room measurement shows per-wait board manifest bytes exceeding ~1KB mean under real (non-synthetic) traffic, or any room again announces a tally whose denominator differs from the evaluator's electorate.
- RE-TARGET: none — no settled axis contradicted; consensus-requires-scrutiny (2+ gate), identity-is-the-connection, proposal-is-a-document and done-means-independently-verified were respected and reaffirmed by the adopted text.
