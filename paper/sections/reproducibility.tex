\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

The repository's \texttt{paper/README.md} gives the commands for inspecting the five-arm snapshot, regenerating tables and figures, auditing prose numbers, and building the manuscript. The two study specifications are \texttt{paper/protocol.md}, \texttt{paper/prereg-arm-k.md}, and \texttt{paper/prereg-confirmatory.md}; dated deviations and exclusions are in \texttt{paper/amendments.md}. Those records distinguish a planned comparison from a later analysis.


For Study 1, the generated A--C family table is a post-data family analysis, while the original per-task tests remain in the suite report. The arm-K report keeps its pre-registered six-test Holm family; the additional pooled interpreter numbers are explicitly exploratory. For Study 2, the generated report prints descriptive cells and the inferential samples separately. The latter remove the mixed-account seeds and printf seed 501, and distinguish the registered short-thinking primary from the later long-thinking tests. Reading one descriptive denominator as an inferential denominator changes the question.

The outcome artifacts are under \texttt{bench/results/rq1-suite}, \texttt{bench/results/rq1-arm-k}, and \texttt{bench/results/rq1-confirmatory}. The sanitized account switch times are in \texttt{bench/results/account-switch-log/switches.json}. Generated reports in \texttt{paper/generated} retain the per-arm denominators, prices, strata, and warnings used here. Study 1's account-window qualification follows the logged switch time; the earlier local-time estimate in the amendments was superseded. For the room-correction tallies, \texttt{docs/arm-c-process-trace.md} states the manual criteria and names representative artifacts.

The manuscript inputs generated tables without editing them. \texttt{paper/regen-manifest.sh} names the generators and their committed inputs; \texttt{scripts/paper-number-audit.ts} checks every typed decimal, fraction, and percentage in the section sources against those inputs and records. The audit confirms provenance of a printed number, not its interpretation. Its source list includes the manual process-trace note only for the reported lower-bound room tallies. The cost-table generator also places the K collection window before the switch time from the logged record; regenerated table rows were not edited to make that correction. Re-running the five-arm report should preserve its missing, mixed-account, and excluded rows rather than silently filling or pooling them.
