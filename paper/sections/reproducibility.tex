\section{Pre-registration, deviations and reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

\paragraph{What was fixed in advance.} The Study 1 A--C comparison and its per-task test were specified before any Study 1 run. The vote arm (K), its group sizes, selectors, per-attempt caps and six-test Holm family were specified after the A and C results but before any K run. Study 2's five arms, seeds, rotation of arm order within each seed, thinking-token threshold, fourteen-test Holm family and predictions were specified before any Study 2 cell existed.

\paragraph{Deviations, each recorded when it happened.} In Study 1, the A--C test was changed after the data to a family-pooled Fisher test, and the printf task version was chosen after both piloted versions had been seen (Section~\ref{sec:method}). In Study 2: the single-agent runaway caps, which bound at once on printf under long thinking, were raised from 0.30~USD and 150~s to 1.50~USD and 900~s before any further printf cell ran, and printf seed 501 was kept out of inference; when the active account hit its usage limit, cells answered by the client's quota message were excluded by a rule that did not look at outcomes and were re-run; once the account effect was found, the remaining cells were launched only while the long-thinking account was active; seeds whose cells spanned two thinking regimes were reported separately; the long-thinking comparisons were added after those outcomes had been seen and are exploratory; and the run was stopped at 190/200 cells by the maintainer, not by a rule.

\paragraph{How the numbers were produced.} Every table and figure is generated by scripts from per-run result records; none is edited by hand, and a regeneration check fails if a regenerated table or figure differs from the one included. Account assignment joins each run's start and end times to a sanitized extract of the account switcher's log that holds only switch times and account slot numbers. The room-correction counts in Section~\ref{sec:results-study1} are manual lower-bound tallies of the seats' final summaries against fixed criteria. An automated check confirms that every decimal, fraction and percentage in the prose also occurs in the generated tables or the study records; it does not check whole numbers, and it does not establish that a matching number supports the sentence it appears in.

\paragraph{Availability.} The system source, task fixtures, per-run result records, generation scripts, pre-registrations and dated deviation record are archived with the project repository at commit \texttt{28961b2} and the manuscript branch built on it.
