\section{Pre-registration, deviations and reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

\paragraph{What was fixed in advance.} The Study 1 A--C comparison and its per-task test were specified before any Study 1 run. The vote arm (K), its group sizes, selectors, per-attempt caps and six-test Holm family were specified after the A and C results but before any K run. Study 2's five arms, seeds, rotation of arm order within each seed, thinking-token threshold, fourteen-test Holm family and predictions were specified before any Study 2 cell existed.

\paragraph{Recorded changes.} Before the Study 1 grid, the original task suite was replaced following screening, and the long-brief task was retired. The admission rule was tightened after earlier candidates proved near ceiling; the below-0.3 exclusion category was added after observing printf v2. The retained printf pilot and post-data version selection are described in Section~\ref{sec:method}. The original fifteen-seed design with sequential stopping was replaced by forty seeds per task. Dollar and wall-clock ceilings derived from each paired C run replaced token/turn matching; seeds served as bookkeeping labels without controlling sampling or seat assignment. The room size was fixed at three, the default deadline increased from 300 to 900 seconds, turn counts came directly from response JSON, and deadline-killed runs were classified as timeouts and made retryable. K's caps, selectors and six-test family were settled before K collection. The later A--C analysis revision is described in Section~\ref{sec:method}. The auxiliary same-window control was stopped after three runs for cost and resumed at reduced scope.

In Study 2, the printf deadline bound on its first A cell. Caps were then raised for A, AH and every K attempt, and the printf block's spending ceiling increased from 74 to 240 USD. The original timed-out A cell was retained separately and rerun; seed 501's inferential exclusion is described in Section~\ref{sec:method}. Killed cells with partial usage from every seat subsequently counted toward the launch budget at list-price estimates multiplied by 1.5; this did not recover their actual cost. An outcome-blind rule invalidated nine quota-refused cells and the operator-killed B cell at printf seed 517; all were rerun, retaining their known spend. Remaining launches were restricted to account 4. Account-log stratification, mixed-seed exclusions and the post-data long-thinking tests are described in Sections~\ref{sec:method} and~\ref{sec:results-study2}. The switch-log extract was later refreshed to correct resumed-cell assignments. The maintainer stopped after printf seed 518 because further cells were judged unlikely to change the observed lack of differences; this was an outcome-informed stop without a pre-specified stopping rule.

\paragraph{How the numbers were produced.} Every table and figure is generated by scripts from per-run result records; none is edited by hand, and a regeneration check fails if a regenerated table or figure differs from the one included. Account assignment joins each run's start and end times to a sanitized extract of the account switcher's log that holds only switch times and account slot numbers. The manual room-correction tallies are described in Section~\ref{sec:results-study1}. The automated audit checks extracted prose decimals, fractions and percentages for numerical matches in generated outputs and listed records, allowing rounding and complementary counts. It omits bare integers and text inside several formatting and link commands, and does not establish that a numerical match supports the surrounding claim.

\paragraph{Availability.} The system source, task fixtures, per-run result records, generation scripts, pre-registrations and dated deviation record are archived with the project repository at commit \texttt{28961b2} and the manuscript branch built on it.
