\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

The repository's \texttt{paper/README.md} gives the commands for inspecting the five-arm snapshot, regenerating tables and figures, auditing prose numbers, and building the manuscript. The two study specifications are \texttt{paper/protocol.md}, \texttt{paper/prereg-arm-k.md}, and \texttt{paper/prereg-confirmatory.md}; dated deviations and exclusions are in \texttt{paper/amendments.md}. Those records distinguish a planned comparison from a later analysis.



The outcome artifacts are under \texttt{bench/results/rq1-suite}, \texttt{bench/results/rq1-arm-k}, and \texttt{bench/results/rq1-confirmatory}. The sanitized account switch times are in \texttt{bench/results/account-switch-log/switches.json}. Generated reports in \texttt{paper/generated} retain the per-arm denominators, prices, strata, and warnings used here. For the room-correction tallies, \texttt{docs/arm-c-process-trace.md} states the manual criteria and names representative artifacts.

The manuscript inputs generated tables without editing them. \texttt{paper/regen-manifest.sh} names their generators and committed inputs. \texttt{scripts/paper-number-audit.ts} checks whether extracted prose decimals, fractions, and percentages have numeric matches somewhere in its source list, which includes the manual process-trace note. It does not check bare integers or establish that a matched number supports the surrounding claim.
